3.)  Zappos VIP Description

Zappos VIP is a free customer loyalty program that allows participants to earn ”VIP Points” with qualifying purchases on Zappos.com. Participants may also earn VIP Points by participating in Promotional Activities (defined in Section 5) that may be offered through Zappos VIP from time to time. Participants in Zappos VIP may redeem accumulated VIP Points for ”VIP Codes” that can be used toward purchasing merchandise on Zappos.com and may also use the VIP Points for other purposes as may be provided by Zappos from time to time. Additional benefits also may be made available to Zappos VIP members from time to time. These VIP Terms provide a general overview of Zappos VIP. For more information about earning, calculating or redeeming VIP Points, Promotional Activities and other details, please visit our Zappos VIP page .

5.)  Earning VIP Points

You earn VIP Points by making qualifying purchases on Zappos.com or otherwise participating in Promotional Activities as defined below.

Purchases . In order to receive VIP Points for a purchase, your order must be placed in the Zappos Account that is associated with your VIP Membership. You will earn VIP Points based on the total amount paid for your purchase, including any tax and shipping but excluding any purchases to the extent made with VIP Codes, other discount codes or gift cards. For more details about how to earn VIP Points by making qualifying purchases, please visit our Zappos VIP page . Purchases made from 6pm.com or any other websites owned or operated by Zappos.com LLC or its affiliates are not eligible for VIP Points or other Zappos VIP benefits unless otherwise indicated.

Promotional Activities . From time to time, we, or others acting with our permission, may offer you opportunities to earn bonus VIP Points, such as inviting you to engage with Zappos in special promotional events or activities in exchange for VIP Points ( ”Promotional Activities” ). For example, we may offer you points for writing an eligible product review on Zappos.com, or we may offer additional points for certain qualifying purchases.

The number of VIP Points that you can earn each calendar year by participating in Promotional Activities will be capped. For more information about the annual cap, please visit our Zappos VIP FAQ page .

For further details on Promotional Activities please visit our Zappos VIP page . VIP Points for participation in Promotional Activities will be awarded as described in the applicable promotional offer and will be subject to any additional terms set forth in the promotional offer. Some Promotional Activities may limit the amount of VIP Points earned per Promotional Activity. VIP Points for eligible participation in Promotional Activities may take seven (7) or more days to be added to your VIP Membership.

6.)  Redemption of VIP Points

You can redeem VIP Points posted to your VIP Membership for VIP Codes, which can be applied toward future purchases on Zappos.com. For more details on redeeming VIP Points for VIP Codes (including the redemption rate), please visit our Zappos VIP page . VIP Codes are only issued in whole $1 increments with a maximum amount per VIP Code of $100. You can redeem your VIP Points for VIP Codes by logging into your Zappos Account and accessing the My VIP dashboard or by contacting the Zappos Customer VIP Team via email or live chat or by calling 1-866-427-2246. We will use commercially reasonable efforts to send your VIP Codes within 24 hours of your request.

VIP Points accumulated under Zappos VIP and VIP Codes issued when VIP Points are redeemed are promotional in nature and have no cash value. VIP Codes are redeemable for merchandise only and cannot be applied to previous purchases or purchases of gift cards. Your VIP Points, VIP Codes and VIP Membership are personal to you and may not be sold, transferred (by operation of law or otherwise) or assigned to or shared with, family, friends or others, or used by you for any commercial purpose. You are responsible for any federal, state or local taxes applicable to your participation in Zappos VIP.

7.)  Expiration of VIP Points and Codes

VIP Points. EACH NOVEMBER (OR AT OTHER TIMES DURING THE VIP YEAR AT OUR DISCRETION AND WITHOUT NOTICE), WE WILL AUTOMATICALLY CONVERT ALL UNREDEEMED VIP POINTS UNDER YOUR VIP MEMBERSHIP INTO ONE or more VIP CODES. IF FOR ANY REASON ZAPPOS DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY CONVERT UNREDEEMED VIP POINTS, THE VIP points WILL EXPIRE ONE (1) YEAR FROM THE DATE THEY ARE EARNED. AUTOMATICALLY CONVERTED VIP POINTS AND any EXPIRED VIP POINTS ARE DEDUCTED FROM THE VIP POINT BALANCE UNDER YOUR VIP MEMBERSHIP.

VIP Codes. VIP Codes expire NINETY (90) DAYS from the date THEY ARE SENT TO YOU. VIP CODES are subject to the additional TERMS AND RESTRICTIONS contained on or included with the VIP code. SOME VIP CODES MAY NOT BE COMBINED WITH OTHER OFFERS OR PROMOTIONS.

8.)  Impact of Merchandise Returns on Your VIP Points and VIP Codes

If you return merchandise that you purchased with your VIP Codes in accordance with the Zappos.com Return Policy, you will be refunded the amount you paid (excluding the value of the VIP Codes and any other discounts) to the original method of payment, after we process your return in accordance with our Return Policy.  For example, if you placed an order totaling $100 for one item and used a $10 VIP Code, we will refund $90 to your original method of payment. If you purchase multiple items using a single VIP Code, then the value of your VIP Code is applied proportionally to each item, and returns of any one item will result in a refund of the amount you paid for the item, excluding the value of the proportionally applied value of the VIP Code. VIP Codes are for one-time use only. You will not receive a replacement VIP Code for returned merchandise and Zappos will not credit the redeemed VIP Points back to your VIP Membership.

If you cancel a purchase or return your purchase, Zappos will deduct the VIP Points that you were awarded for that purchase from your VIP Membership. If you have already redeemed those VIP Points for a VIP Code, this could potentially result in a negative VIP Point balance under your VIP Membership.
